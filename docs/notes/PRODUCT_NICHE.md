# Product niche

> Locked 2026-10-29 ~18:53 PDT (pure AI — Product + Lead). Crypto / chain tracks scrubbed from product narrative.

## Who
Users who want private local AI / personal research on-device — professional, privacy-first. Primary product path: Android (Google Play or APK). Web is a companion surface; CLI secondary.

## Promise (direction — not all shipped)
- Local-first / on-device by default; fail closed when no local model is connected.
- Notes and chat stay on the device unless the user opts into a labeled remote path (never silent; never default).
- Credentials stay out of the model.
- Refuse secrets; no silent remote.

## Foundation (unchanged)
Privacy / fail-closed stack: local ask path, refusal gates, sanitizer-oriented design. Do not claim live source-identity masking or “local-only” as absolute if a labeled REMOTE path exists — label those paths.

## Out of scope / do not claim yet
Live Play listing; on-phone model as a shipping product; live source-ID masking; attested / verified remote; BYOK as default or silent fallback; Android app as shipped.

## Scrubbed from product narrative
Domain-specific chain / token / wallet product tracks — including as “optional” framing — are not part of the hero, niche, About, or STATUS positioning.
