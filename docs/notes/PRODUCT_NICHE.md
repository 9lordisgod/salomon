# Product niche — Salomon (locked)

**Who:** People who want a private local AI / personal research agent on their phone and a web companion — professional, privacy-first.

**Promise:**
- Local-first by default; fail closed when no local model is connected.
- Work stays on-device unless the user opts into a labeled remote path (never silent; never default).
- Credentials never enter the model; refuse secrets.

**Out of scope / do not claim yet:** Live Play Store listing; on-device phone inference as a shipping product; live source-identity masking; attested remote; BYOK as default or silent fallback.

Prior domain-specific product tracks — including as “optional” framing — are not part of the hero, niche, About, or STATUS positioning.
