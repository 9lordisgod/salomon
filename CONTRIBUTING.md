# Contributing

Salomon is a **private, proprietary** repository owned by Anon Rothschild.
External contributions are not accepted unless the Owner has granted written
permission and a contributor agreement is in place.

## Internal contributors (Salomon AI team)

1. Keep the professional niche: investigative journalists, private investigators,
   and researchers; local-first; fail-closed; no credentials in the model.
2. Do not claim unbuilt features (M2 masking, M3 export UX, Play listing,
   on-device phone inference) as shipped.
3. Prefer small, reviewed tips on `main`. No force-push to `main` without Lead OK.
4. Secrets, real keys, user PII, and source material must never enter the repo,
   commits, or model context.
5. Charts and UI: zinc monochrome (black / white / grey) only — see
   `docs/notes/CHART_STYLE.md`.
6. License: all new files inherit proprietary ownership unless the Owner says
   otherwise in writing. Do not add SPDX identifiers that imply an open license.
